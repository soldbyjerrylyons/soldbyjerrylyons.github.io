What Rates Are Doing

The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate is 7.40% as of October 8, 2026, according to Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey — up from 7.28% the week before and 6.30% a year ago. The 15-year fixed averaged 6.73%. Your own rate will land above or below that depending on credit, down payment, and loan type. Rates are now above 7%, so if you were pre-approved even a month or two ago, the payment on the same purchase price has changed and it's worth getting refreshed numbers from your lender.

If You're Selling

  • Baltimore County's median days on market was 32 in September, up from 29 in August. Well-priced homes still move; overpriced ones sit long enough for buyers to notice.
  • The field is getting more crowded. Baltimore County had 2.7 months of supply in September, up from 2.2 in August, and as of October 14 inventory had reached 3.2 months versus 2.5 a year ago. Your home is being compared against more options than it was last fall.
  • The county's median sold price was $365,000 in September, down from $388,500 in August. With rates above 7%, buyers are working from tighter budgets. Price to what's selling now, not to this summer's comps.

If You're Buying

  • At 7.40%, you're paying more than a buyer who locked a year ago at 6.30%. If your pre-approval is more than a few weeks old, get it re-run before you write an offer — your real budget may have moved.
  • You have more choice than last fall. Baltimore County had 2,003 homes in inventory as of October 14 — 3.2 months of supply, up from 1,778 homes and 2.5 months a year ago — and 1,002 new listings came on in September.
  • Homes are taking a bit longer to sell (32 median days in September, up from 29 in August). That can leave room to negotiate on price, inspections, closing help, and timing — especially on homes that have been sitting.
