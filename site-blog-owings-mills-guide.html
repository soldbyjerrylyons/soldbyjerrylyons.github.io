Owings Mills, MD Homes for Sale & Open Houses: A Local Guide

Where Owings Mills is, how to get around, what's nearby, what the housing looks like, and how to find open houses and homes for sale.

By Jerry Lyons, REALTOR® with Next Step Realty · November 2026

Owings Mills is one of the first places buyers look when they search the northwest side of Baltimore County. It has the end of the Metro line, a lot of newer housing, and a big state park on its western edge. Here's a plain guide to where it is, how to get around, what's nearby, and how to see homes, including upcoming open houses.

Where Owings Mills Is

Owings Mills is in northwest Baltimore County. Baltimore County has no incorporated cities or towns, so Owings Mills has no town government of its own. County government handles zoning, roads, parks, and libraries, and the U.S. Census Bureau tracks it as a census-designated place.

That matters when you shop. An "Owings Mills" mailing address (ZIP code 21117) doesn't follow one official town line, so confirm the exact location, tax details, and school assignment for each address you're considering.

Getting Around

  • I-795 (the Northwest Expressway) runs through Owings Mills. The state is in the engineering stage on a new I-795 interchange at Dolfield Boulevard.
  • Reisterstown Road (MD 140) is the older main road through the area, with shopping and services along it.
  • Owings Mills Boulevard (MD 940) and Red Run Boulevard connect the newer neighborhoods, offices, and shopping.
  • Metro: Owings Mills is the northwest end of MDOT MTA's Metro SubwayLink, which runs to downtown Baltimore and Johns Hopkins. Schedules change, so check MDOT MTA before you count on a train commute.

As with any area, drive or ride your real commute on a weekday at the time you'd actually travel.

Parks and Everyday Places

  • Soldiers Delight Natural Environment Area (5100 Deer Park Road): Maryland DNR lists about 1,900 acres of serpentine barrens, more than 39 rare, threatened, or endangered plant species, and 7 miles of marked hiking trails. No bikes or horses.
  • Northwest Regional Park (4515 Deer Park Road): a Baltimore County park of about 323 acres at Lyons Mill and Deer Park Roads, with a Ravens-themed playground near the Lyons Mill Road entrance and part of the Locust Run stream greenway.
  • Metro Centre at Owings Mills: the Owings Mills Branch of the Baltimore County Public Library (10302 Grand Central Avenue) shares the building with the Community College of Baltimore County's CCBC Owings Mills location, next to the Metro station. The library lists free garage parking.

Hours and facilities change, so check each official site before you visit.

What the Housing Looks Like

The Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2020–2024 estimates) counts about 15,700 homes in the Owings Mills census area:

  • About one in five are detached single-family homes.
  • About one in four are attached single-family homes, such as townhouses.
  • A little over half are in buildings with two or more units, mostly apartment and condo buildings.

The housing here is newer than in much of the Baltimore area. About two-thirds of homes were built in 1990 or later, and fewer than one in ten were built before 1960. Those counts include rentals as well as owner-occupied homes. In practice, a search here turns up a lot of townhomes and condos, newer single-family subdivisions, and some older homes closer to Reisterstown Road.

Newer doesn't mean you can skip the inspection. Ask about the age of the roof and HVAC, and on a townhome or condo, read the HOA or condo documents and fees before you commit.

How to Find Open Houses in Owings Mills

  • My open houses: the Open Houses page lists the public open houses I'm hosting and is refreshed every Tuesday.
  • Owings Mills listings: search Owings Mills homes for sale through Next Step Realty. Open house times show on the listings that have them.
  • Confirm before you go. Open houses get canceled when a home goes under contract, so check the listing the morning of.

Two things to know when you walk in. In Maryland, the agent hosting an open house can meet the state's agency disclosure rule by posting a notice that they represent the seller, so assume they work for the seller. And you don't need to sign a buyer agreement just to talk with an agent at an open house. If you want an agent to take you on a private tour, a written buyer agreement comes first.

Schools

Owings Mills is served by Baltimore County Public Schools. I don't rate or rank schools. Check boundaries and school information for a specific address at bcps.org.
